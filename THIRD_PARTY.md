Priel.app carries `juist` and `juistd` built from [0x23me/juist](https://github.com/0x23me/juist),
a fork of juist with three small commits on the `priel` branch. `opnsense/tools/mkpkg` is juist's `packaging/freebsd/mkpkg`, changed in one line.
juist's license and its third-party licenses are in its repository at the version in
`mac/juist/VERSION`.

its `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES`.
